What works

The light

The screen's edges glow in colours picked from the cover, flowing around the rim and breathing with the bass, every sung word, or the room through your mic.

Lyrics word by word

Stacked bubbles or one big line. Word timing from LRCLIB, the free lyrics library; line-timed songs get their words spread out.

It plays the song

Search a song and it plays on the page in YouTube's own player, the lyrics locked to it. Or paste a YouTube link.

Anything, anywhere

Playing it in Spotify or on a record? Hit Start as it starts, or tap the line you're hearing. ← → nudges the timing, remembered per song.

Your song files

MP3, M4A, FLAC, WAV, OGG: exact timing, tags and cover read in the browser. Nothing is uploaded.

Follow Spotify or Apple Music

One command on your Mac or Windows PC and the lyrics keep up with your player, song after song. It only answers your own browser.

Full screen, floating lyrics

F for full screen, a floating lyrics window over your other apps in Chrome and Edge, and the screen stays awake.

Not yet

  • On phones the light glows around the tab, not around everything else you have open.
  • The Windows helper isn't tested on a Windows PC yet; the Mac helper isn't tested with Spotify playing yet.
  • No lyrics editor or translations. Lyrics only exist for songs LRCLIB has.

Run it yourself

cd backlit && npm install && npm run dev
